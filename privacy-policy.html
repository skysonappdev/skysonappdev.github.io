Privacy policy

Synth Runner, by SkysonDevApper
Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy covers the Synth Runner game for Android: what it collects, what stays on your phone, and what goes to the services it uses.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. Your settings, scores, levels and songs stay on your phone.
  • If you choose to share your best runs, they join an anonymous online top 10 for other players to race. You can delete them at any time.
  • If you use Google Play Games, your scores, achievements and progress are kept on your Play Games account.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to show and measure them. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, you're asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time under AD PRIVACY OPTIONS on the menu.

What stays on your phone

All of this is kept by the game on your device. We never see it.

  • Settings:
    • the look and effects you choose
    • controls
    • language
    • the name shown on the title
  • Progress:
    • best scores and run times
    • your own ghost runs, the replays you race against
    • top-10 runs downloaded from the online top 10, so you can race them offline
  • Levels:
    • levels you make in the level editor
    • levels you open from a file
  • Songs:
    • songs you pick for SONG mode or for a level
    • The game reads the song file you choose, through Android's own file picker, and works out its beats on your phone.
    • It keeps those beats on the phone, so it doesn't have to work them out again.
    • The song itself is never uploaded.

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes all of this.

Online top 10 (Google Firebase)

Each mode has an online top 10 of runs that every player can race against as ghosts. Sharing your own runs is your choice: the game asks once, and you can turn SHARE MY BEST RUNS on or off at any time in the ghost list.

When sharing is on and a run would make a mode's top 10, the game sends:

  • the score, the mode and the version of its course
  • a recording of the run: where you were on the course over time, and where you hit something
  • the time it was sent
  • a random player ID that Google Firebase creates for the game. It has no name, email address or account behind it.

Other players download top-10 runs to race against them. Runs are shown under a made-up name, such as NOVA-48, never your own name. The runs are stored by Google Cloud Firestore, part of Google Firebase.

Deleting your runs: tap DELETE MY SHARED RUNS in the ghost list. Your runs are removed from every top 10, and sharing turns off. Your best runs on your phone stay.

How Google handles Firebase data: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

SONG runs and levels you make or open are never sent.

Google Play Games

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game uses it for:

  • Leaderboards for TUBE (EASY, MEDIUM, HARD and EXPERT), PLANE (EASY, MEDIUM and HARD), CHAOS, WILDCARD and VARIETY
  • Achievements
  • A cloud save of your progress (best scores, achievement progress and your best ghost run for each mode), so it follows you to other devices signed in to the same account

Google then handles your Play Games profile (gamer name and player ID), your scores and your saved progress, under Google's privacy policy.

Google Play

Google Play may show a card asking you to rate the game, and the game asks Google Play whether a newer version is available. Both are handled by Google Play.

What you choose to share or save

  • Share: the share button makes a picture of your score and hands it to the app you pick, such as a messaging app. That app's own privacy policy then applies.
  • Saving files: saving a level or your setup writes a file where you choose, or to your Downloads folder. It stays on your phone.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows banner ads on its menus and, now and then, a full-screen ad between runs. Ads never show during a run. The ads come from Google AdMob.

To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect the following:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be worked out
  • device and app information
  • your interactions with ads
  • diagnostic data

Your choices:

  • Consent: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks before personalised ads are shown. Choose AD PRIVACY OPTIONS on the menu to change your answer at any time.
  • Advertising ID: on any Android phone, you can reset or delete it under Settings → Privacy → Ads (the wording varies by phone).

How Google uses this data is explained at:

  • https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Permissions

The game asks for one permission: vibration, for haptic feedback. It doesn't use your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

It also uses internet access, for the online top 10, Google Play Games and the ads, and the ads library uses the advertising ID, as described above.

Security

We don't run servers of our own. The online top 10 uses Google Firebase, and everything the game and Google's services send goes over encrypted connections.

Deleting your data

Synth Runner, by SkysonDevApper

Synth Runner has no accounts. This page explains how to delete the little data the game sends online, and what happens to the rest.

Your runs in the online top 10

If you shared your best runs, delete them from inside the game:

  1. Open Synth Runner and tap GHOST on the menu to open the ghost list.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap DELETE MY SHARED RUNS.
  3. Tap Delete to confirm.

What is deleted: every run you shared, on every top 10, with its score, recording and the random player ID it was sent under. They are removed at once, and sharing turns off.

What is kept: nothing online. The best runs stored on your own phone stay, so you can keep racing them.

Can't open the game any more? Email us at the address below and say roughly when you played and which modes; we'll remove matching runs.

Google Play Games

Leaderboard scores, achievements and the cloud save of your progress belong to your Google Play Games profile. Delete them in the Play Games app or at myaccount.google.com (Data and privacy → Play Games). Google handles this under its own privacy policy.

Everything on your phone

Your settings, scores, levels and songs never leave your phone. Uninstall the game, or clear its storage in Android's settings, to delete them.

Ads

Ad data is collected by Google AdMob. Reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Privacy → Ads on your phone (the wording varies by phone). See Google's privacy policy.
